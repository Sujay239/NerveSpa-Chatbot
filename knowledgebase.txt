Device Troubleshooting Guides
Nerve Bath Device
For a Frozen Device:
Ensure the lock-button icon is unlocked.
Verify that lead wires and carbon rubber pads are connected, one pad is in each water bay, and feet are inserted, as intensity requires user engagement.
Ensure the touch screen is not touched with wet hands.
Use a light touch/tap on the screen, avoiding pressing too hard.
Confirm the device was not dropped in water.
If issues persist, shut down the device, charge it for 24 hours, and restart.
For No or Weak Stimulation:
Verify all lead wire and pad connections, and that feet are inserted.
If late-stage neuropathy is suspected, test the stimulation by inserting hands into the bays with intensity up to confirm the device is working, then avoid overstimulating the feet.
Add more salt to the water.
Replace lead wires if the device is over 6 months old.
For Device Not Powering On:
Charge the device for 24 hours.
Hold down the power button on the side of the device for 3-5 seconds.
For Stimulation Stops or Program Ends Short of 30 Minutes:
Charge the device for 24 hours.
LED Wrap
For Flickering or Early Powering Down:
Ensure the cord is firmly connected to the wall and into the power brick.
Treatment Mode:
Use the blue mode (second setting) for both red and infrared lights; the green mode (first setting) only powers the red lights.
Important: Never crease the LED wrap by folding it in half and applying pressure.
Knee Pro Device
Hold the power button down to turn on or off; tap it to change modes (the device automatically cycles from Mode 1 to Mode 2).
If stimulation issues occur, charge the device for 24 hours, ensuring the two metal prongs are correctly inserted into the corresponding holes on the back of the device.
Plug the leadwire pigtails in firmly and replace gel pads as needed.
Quake Plate
Do not stand on the Quake Plate.
If a loud plastic-on-plastic grinding sound is heard, turn the device off and push the top plate horizontally away from the noise source. Repeat if necessary and press in the middle of the plate with hands or feet while listening for noise improvement.
